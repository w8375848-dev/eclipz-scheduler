Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

eCLIPZ Scheduler ("the app") is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer.

What the app accesses

When an account is connected, the app receives the access tokens and basic profile information (such as display name, avatar and account ID) that the platform provides, and basic statistics about the account's own videos.

How it is used

This information is used only to publish videos to the connected account and show their status to the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Tokens and post data are stored locally on the owner's computer. They can be removed at any time by disconnecting the account in the app or revoking access in the platform's settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app owner through the contact details on their connected social media profiles.
